Governing relations
(1)ln Pr = A(1 − 1/Tr) ,  A = 7
(2)P7,8 = Psat(T8) — the CC fixes the loop pressure level
(3)P1 − P9 = ΔPGV + ΔPVL + ΔPCOND + ΔPLL + ΔPWICK
(4)ΔPcap ≤ ΔPcap,max = 2σ(T8)/rp
(5)T1 = Tsat(P1) ,  T1 − T8 ≈ ΔPext (dT/dP)sat
(6)ṁ = Q/hfg ,  ΔPv ∝ ṁ/ρv(T8)
(7)Q = UA2φ(T5 − Tsink) ⇒ L2φ/Lc
(8)Qleak = ṁ cp,l ΔTsub — CC energy balance
(9)ΔTsub,av = (T5 − Tsink)[1 − exp(−NTUsub)]
(10)hfg* = θ0.38, σ* = θ1.26, ρl* = θ0.28, θ = (1−Tr)/0.3
Qualitative corresponding-states closure: ρv* ∝ Pr/Tr (ideal gas), μl* = exp[2.5(1/Tr − 1/0.7)], all ΔP laminar (∝ ṁμ/ρ), cp,l* = 2.5, cp,v* = 1.4. Segment 2–3 and 3–4 carry vapour superheat; 5–6 and 6–7 carry liquid subcooling. Pressure and temperature scales in Fig. 2 are magnified about the operating point, as in the schematic construction.
